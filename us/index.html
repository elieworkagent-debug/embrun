Why audio is the right tool here

  • The test is oral. You can read all 128 answers and still freeze when a person asks you question 17. Listening forces you to retrieve the answer instead of recognising it on a page.
  • You can practise while doing something else. Commute, dishes, gym: the half hour a day that is otherwise lost.
  • You hear the hard words. Representatives, Constitution, Executive branch, Emancipation Proclamation, Electoral College — pronouncing them badly is a real risk in an oral interview.
  • Six seconds of silence is the training. Every question gives you exactly the gap you need to answer out loud before the answer is spoken.

What is in the 2025 test

USCIS gives the 2025 version of the civics test to people who file for naturalization on or after 20 October 2025. There are 128 questions in the bank, the officer asks up to 20, and you need 12 correct to pass. The officer stops as soon as you reach 12 correct or 9 wrong. The questions are grouped in the USCIS list as American Government (72 questions), American History (46) and Symbols and Holidays (10).

If you are 65 or older and have been a permanent resident for 20 years or more, you study only the 20 questions marked with an asterisk (*), are asked 10 and need 6. Those questions are flagged throughout this kit.

Questions

Is this a USCIS product?

No. The questions and answers are the M-1778 list published by USCIS, which is public domain; the audio, practice tests, explanations and design are ours. This kit is independent and is not endorsed by USCIS. Everything the government publishes is free on uscis.gov — you are paying for the listening version and the practice tests, not for access to the questions.

Does it work for the 65/20 exception?

Yes. The 20 starred questions are marked with * in the PDF and read in the same audio files.

What if the answers about the President change?

Four questions (President, Vice President, Speaker, Chief Justice) and four state-dependent ones have no fixed answer. They are marked as changing in every file, with the link to check them. The other 120 answers are stable.
