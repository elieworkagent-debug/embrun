Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2025

This website, embrun.store, sells digital study packs. The seller is an individual, Elias Bellouti — operator of Embrun — contactable at eliework@embrun.store.

1. Who processes your data

The seller operates this website and decides why and how personal data is used (data controller). Payments are processed by Creem, which acts as Merchant of Record: it is the legal seller of the transaction, handles card data, and collects applicable taxes.

2. What we collect

  • Email address — only when you contact us, or when you provide it at checkout for delivery of your download.
  • Payment data — collected and stored by Creem, never by this website. We do not see or keep your card number.
  • Technical data — the web host records standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL) for security and abuse prevention.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics on this site.

3. Why we use it

  • Deliver the files you purchased.
  • Answer your questions and provide support.
  • Meet accounting and tax obligations, through Creem.
  • Keep the site secure and prevent fraud.

The legal bases are: performance of a contract (delivery), legitimate interest (security, support), and legal obligation (accounting).

4. How long we keep it

Support emails: up to 3 years after the last exchange. Transaction records: kept by Creem for the period required by tax law. Server logs: 30 days maximum.

5. Who receives it

Creem (payments and tax), the web hosting provider (serving the pages), and the email provider routing messages to eliework@embrun.store. No data is sold, rented or shared for marketing.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to processing, or restrict it. Write to eliework@embrun.store — we answer within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your national authority (in France: CNIL).

7. Transfers outside the EU

Payment processing may involve transfers outside the European Union, covered by the safeguards Creem applies under the GDPR. Email and hosting providers may process data outside the EU under standard contractual clauses.
